Our Vision

"In an era scarred by conflict, our vision is one of enduring peace for all humanity. C.O.M.P.S. was founded on the principle that through vigilant Observation, impartial Evaluation, and swift intervention, a lasting Peace can be achieved. We are a unified force, comprising the best from the Earth Alliance, ZAFT, and the Orb Union, dedicated to transcending old hatreds. Our solemn duty is to monitor global tensions, mediate disputes, and act as the shield for the innocent. We envision a future where cooperation triumphs over confrontation, securing a brighter tomorrow for generations to come."
